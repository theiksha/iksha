Section 1

1. Background

India’s civilisational heritage encompasses a vast and interconnected body of knowledge — spanning philosophy, linguistics, mathematics, astronomy, medicine, governance, agriculture, and the arts — developed and refined over millennia.

The Indian Knowledge Systems and Heritage Association (IKSHA), in partnership with the Indian Institute of Technology Bombay (IITB), has been convening an annual academic conference to bring scholars, practitioners, and institutions together to critically examine, document, and advance the study of these traditions using rigorous, contemporary academic methods.

3rd

Building on the success of its first two editions (AAC 2025 and AAC 2026), IKSHA is pleased to announce the 3rd Annual Academic Conference — IKSHA 2027 / AAC 2027 — to be held from 2 to 4 July 2027 at IIT Bombay.

Section 2

2. About the Conference

IKSHA 2027 is a three-day, multi-track academic conference dedicated to the interdisciplinary study of Indian Knowledge Systems (IKS).

It aims to provide a rigorous scholarly platform where classical Indic frameworks — in philosophy, linguistics, science, medicine, statecraft, agriculture, and the arts — are studied on their own terms, examined against contemporary disciplinary methods, and explored for their continuing relevance.

Core Purpose

3. Objectives

Establishing a disciplined, sustained ecosystem for research, critique, and scholarship in Indian Knowledge Systems.

01

Interdisciplinary Academic Platform

To create a rigorous academic platform for the interdisciplinary study of Indian Knowledge Systems across philosophy, linguistics, science, medicine, governance, agriculture, and the arts.

02

Original Textual Reconstruction

To encourage original research that reconstructs, documents, and critically evaluates classical Indic texts, traditions, and practices.

03

Dialogue Between Śāstra & Modern Disciplines

To foster dialogue between traditional scholarship (śāstra) and contemporary academic and scientific disciplines.

04

Forum for Emerging Scholars

To provide early-career researchers, doctoral scholars, and students a forum to present peer-reviewed work and receive expert feedback.

05

Global Collaborative Networks

To strengthen collaborative networks among universities, research institutions, and practitioners working on Indian Knowledge Systems, in India and abroad.

06

High-Quality Scholarly Proceedings

To publish high-quality conference proceedings that contribute lasting scholarly resources to the field.

Author Instructions

9. Manuscript Preparation Guidelines

Please review the submission formats, review policies, and academic integrity guidelines thoroughly prior to submitting your work.

Paper Submission

  • Submit the full paper along with an extended abstract.
  • Manuscripts must be submitted in PDF format.
  • The manuscript must be original, unpublished, and not under consideration elsewhere.
  • AI Policy: Use of AI tools is strictly limited to formatting and paraphrasing only.

Manuscript Format

  • Format: A4 page size, single spacing, 12-point Times New Roman font, standard margins.
  • Author Details: Full name, designation, institutional affiliation, email address, and mobile number.
  • Do not include any additional biographical information.
  • Language: Papers must be written in English.

Review Process & Standards

  • All submissions will undergo rigorous peer review by the Conference Review Committee.
  • Authors may be asked to revise manuscripts based on reviewer comments prior to final acceptance.
  • Papers should demonstrate originality, academic rigor, and adherence to ethical research practices.
  • Manuscripts not complying with prescribed guidelines may be rejected without review.

Copyright & Presentation

  • Authors must submit a signed Copyright Declaration Form along with the final manuscript.
  • Submitted work must be original and free from plagiarism.
  • Only accepted papers will be scheduled for presentation.
  • Each accepted paper will be allotted either an oral presentation slot or a poster slot.
