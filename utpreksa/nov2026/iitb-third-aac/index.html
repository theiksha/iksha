IIT Bombay to Host Three-Day Conference on Indian Knowledge Systems in July 2027

IKSHA and IIT Bombay partner to organize the 3rd Annual Academic Conference (AAC 2027) across 10 interdisciplinary thematic tracks at the Powai campus in Mumbai.

Event Dates July 2–4, 2027 Three-Day Academic Conference
Venue IIT Bombay Powai Campus, Mumbai
Submission Deadline November 30, 2026 First-Round Full Paper & Abstract
Organisers IKSHA & IIT Bombay Secretariat: conference@theiksha.org

MUMBAI: The Indian Knowledge Systems and Heritage Association (IKSHA), in collaboration with the Indian Institute of Technology Bombay (IIT Bombay), will organize the third Annual Academic Conference on Indian Knowledge Systems from July 2 to 4, 2027, at the institute’s Powai campus in Mumbai.

The three-day conference, titled AAC 2027, will bring together researchers, academics, students and practitioners to examine India’s traditional knowledge systems through interdisciplinary research and contemporary academic methods, according to the organizers’ announcement. The programme will cover 10 thematic areas spanning philosophy, history, linguistics, governance, health, mathematics, astronomy, agriculture, ecology, arts, architecture and engineering.

The conference is intended to provide a platform for original research, critical evaluation of classical texts and dialogue between traditional scholarship and modern academic disciplines. The organizers also aim to expand collaboration among universities, research institutions and practitioners working in the field.

Call for Papers & Submission Guidelines

The first-round submission deadline is November 30, 2026. Authors are required to submit a full paper alongside an extended abstract in PDF format. Submissions must be original, unpublished and not under consideration elsewhere. Papers must be written in English and will undergo review by the conference review committee.

Further information on submissions, registration and academic partnerships is available on the official AAC 2027 website. Queries may be directed to the conference secretariat at conference@theiksha.org.
