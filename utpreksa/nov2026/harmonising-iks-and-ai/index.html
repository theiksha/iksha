Harmonising IKS and AI: The Dawn of a New Era

IKSHA launches its flagship Talk Series at Jawaharlal Nehru University (JNU), New Delhi, exploring the evolving relationship between Indian Knowledge Systems, emerging technologies, and the human edge in the AI age.

Date of Event September 16, 2026
Venue JNU, New Delhi
Keynote Speaker Shri Nitin Seth Co-founder & CEO, Incedo
Audience ~150 Participants Faculty, Researchers & Students

NEW DELHI: IKSHA launched its Talk Series with an inaugural programme titled “Harmonising IKS and AI: The Dawn of a New Era” at Jawaharlal Nehru University (JNU), New Delhi, on September 16, 2026. The initiative seeks to foster dialogue on contemporary issues relating to Indian Knowledge Systems (IKS), emerging technologies and the evolving relationship between human beings and Artificial Intelligence (AI).

Shri Nitin Seth, Co-founder and CEO of Incedo, delivered the inaugural talk, exploring the rapidly evolving AI landscape and its implications for humanity, work and society. Drawing on his presentation, Human Edge in the AI Age, he emphasized that the true promise of AI lies not merely in developing increasingly capable machines, but also in enabling human beings to cultivate wisdom, judgement, creativity, consciousness and meaningful problem-solving.

“The true promise of AI lies not merely in developing increasingly capable machines, but also in enabling human beings to cultivate wisdom, judgement, creativity, consciousness and meaningful problem-solving.”

— Shri Nitin Seth, Co-founder & CEO, Incedo

A key focus of the address was the changing nature of work and the opportunities emerging in the AI era. While AI is expected to transform many existing roles, Seth highlighted the growing scope for entrepreneurship, innovation and new forms of work as the technology increasingly takes over routine and cognitive tasks. He stressed that organizations must rethink their workflows and approaches to value creation rather than treat AI merely as another technological tool.

The talk also explored the human and philosophical dimensions of technological advancement. Connecting the evolving capabilities of AI with different dimensions of the human self, Shri Seth underscored the need to nurture qualities that distinguish human beings. He presented his POSSIBLE Framework as a practical roadmap for developing the “human edge” in an AI-driven world.

ROADMAP FOR THE HUMAN EDGE

The POSSIBLE Framework

Presented by Shri Nitin Seth to anchor human excellence, consciousness, and leadership alongside technological expansion:

P

Problem-solving

Cultivating analytical rigour and first-principles reasoning.

O

Openness

Embracing intellectual humility and multidisciplinary curiosity.

S

Spirituality

Rooting intellect in consciousness, introspection, and inner harmony.

S

Sports

Nurturing resilience, active discipline, and physical well-being.

I

Impact

Directing knowledge and innovation towards purposeful societal benefit.

B

Balance

Sustaining equilibrium across work, mind, life, and technology.

L

Leadership

Inspiring ethical conviction, responsibility, and collective vision.

E

Entrepreneurship

Creating novel value, pioneering initiatives, and bold agency.

Concluding his address, Shri Seth reflected on the choices facing humanity as AI continues to advance. He called for a conscious, purposeful and responsible approach to technological change, emphasizing that AI should be harnessed not merely to improve efficiency but also to promote human development and contribute to a more meaningful future.

Distinguished Gathering & Participation

The programme was attended by around 150 participants, including distinguished guests, faculty members, research scholars and students.
